**Academic Planner 0.1.1** adds a dark appearance and a clearer dashboard calendar. Your data and
settings carry over from 0.1.0 unchanged: install over the previous version and open the app.

## New

### Light, Dark, or System appearance
- A three-way switch (monitor, sun, moon) in the top-right corner of every page.
- **System** follows your computer's light/dark setting as it changes; **Light** and **Dark** fix
  the look regardless.
- The whole app follows it: pages, buttons, checkboxes, drop-downs, status labels, the calendar,
  and the window's title bar.
- The dark palette keeps the app's warm, paper-like character (charcoal page, cream text, a softer
  terracotta accent), and every text color meets the WCAG AA contrast level.
- Your choice is remembered and included in backups; restoring a backup applies its appearance.
  Backups made with 0.1.0 restore as System.

### A clearer dashboard calendar
- **Holidays and exam periods** (midterm and final weeks entered as holidays) are tinted, and
  clicking one shows its name in the day panel, e.g. "Holiday: Midterm week".
- **Weekends** are lightly shaded.
- **Assessment due days** get a small red dot under the date.
- Class days no longer carry a marker; click a day to see its classes and update their status.
- A legend under the calendar explains each highlight.

## Installing or updating

The builds are not signed, so your system may warn you the first time:

- **macOS**: open the `.dmg` and drag Academic Planner to Applications, replacing the old copy.
  - If macOS says the app **is damaged and can't be opened**, it is not damaged. Click **Cancel**
    (not Move to Trash), then run this in Terminal:
    `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Academic Planner.app"`
  - If macOS says it cannot verify the developer, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and
    click **Open Anyway** (on older macOS, right-click the app and choose **Open**).
- **Windows**: run the installer; if SmartScreen appears, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux**: `sudo apt install ./academic-planner_*_amd64.deb`, or make the AppImage executable with
  `chmod +x` and run it.

Your planner data stays where it was (the folder is shown in Settings). Full steps are in the
[README](https://github.com/sahil0319/Academic-Planner#download-and-install).

## Known limitations

- The macOS build is for Apple Silicon only; Intel Macs can build from source.
- Notifications appear only while the app is open.
- The installers are not code-signed.
